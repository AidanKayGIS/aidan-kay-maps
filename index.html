Aidan Kay Maps is an independent cartographic design studio exploring the meeting point of geography, design and memory.

Each composition draws on the natural forms of the landscape: contours, shorelines and the quiet character of a place. Designed to sit beautifully on a wall and make somewhere meaningful feel a little closer.
