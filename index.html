ABOUT / AIDAN KAY MAPS

A small studio.
A growing atlas.

Aidan Kay Maps is an independent project exploring how maps can become objects worth living with. The collection begins with Scotland’s islands and will grow to cover places people care about.

This site is a work in progress as the first printed collection takes shape.
