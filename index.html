THE APPROACH / FROM DATA TO DESIGN

Start with
the coastline.

Every place begins with a shape: the outline of a bay, an island or a headland. Geography provides the foundation for each design.

The finished topographic editions will combine map data with thoughtful typography, colour and layout developed in QGIS. The studies on this page show that design process taking shape.

  • Geographic coastlines, not invented silhouettes
  • Map design shaped by the landscape

GOOD TO KNOW / BEFORE ORDERING

Every detail matters.

These are artwork previews, not products currently for sale. We’ll publish verified specifications and policies before accepting payments.

Can I buy a map print today?

Not yet. The collection is in development. The previews let you explore the design direction; ordering will open only when the finished artwork and fulfilment are ready.

What sizes and paper will be available?

Final sizes, paper stock and finishes are being assessed. We’ll provide exact dimensions and photographs of physical test prints before launch.

How much will shipping cost?

Delivery charges, destinations and dispatch estimates are not confirmed. They will be stated clearly before checkout is enabled.

What about returns and damaged prints?

A complete returns and damaged-order policy will be published before orders open, alongside the seller’s contact information and applicable statutory rights.

Are these accurate topographic maps?

The current studies use geographic coastline data with illustrative interior linework. They are not finished, data-accurate topographic editions.
